Farcaster vote, counted

On 15 Oct Network School (NS), which is buying Farcaster, asked farcaster.xyz users to vote: stay under its new rules, or leave. A stay vote mints a VOTE token on Base, Coinbase’s blockchain, so stays can be counted in full. A leave vote writes nothing on-chain that I can find. It could only be read through the app’s API, and since 19 Oct the API no longer returns it.

Farcaster counts IDs called fids. One person can hold several, and many belong to bots or were never used. This page counts what can be counted from outside and shows where the count stops. No opinion, just public facts I haven’t seen in one place.

The four overlap: the 263 restricted and the 424 leavers without a token are inside the 99.3%, and 57 of the 481 now hold VOTE. The app’s API reports each fid as opted_in (signed), opted_out (voted to leave), moderated_out (restricted; no reason is given, so spam and conduct cases share it) or pending (no answer, the default).

Disclosure: I voted stay on 16 Oct; NS accepted me on 17 Oct, I haven’t booked yet; I’m in the Telegram of Hypersnap, a fork of the Farcaster node software.

481 is a minimum: most fids with casts above ~1.43M were read only after the change, so more leave votes were likely cast by 19 Oct than I could read. 57 have since signed, 37 of them before the change.

The change was on the server. The app’s opt-in endpoints and the vote page text are the same as on 16 Oct, and none of those endpoints lets a user turn a leave vote into pending. I found no announcement (team casts, /farcaster, web; Hypersnap Telegram to 19 Oct, 15:54 UTC). I can’t tell whether the stored leave votes changed or the API only stopped showing them. A leaver’s own view is untested.

Hub keys read 19 Oct; fids counted 20 Oct

About half of all fids never had a farcaster.xyz key

The vote ran only in farcaster.xyz and needed its key. A key is what lets an app post for a fid. Hubs, the public servers that store every Farcaster message whichever app sent it, record which apps ever held a key for a fid.

Above fid 1.5M, the 12 signers whose first key came from Base App had all added a farcaster.xyz key (census of all 1,841 signers there, 19 Oct). @suji pointed out on 17 Oct that fids 1.5M–3M are likely Base App accounts.

Logged-out reads and public hubs 18–20 Oct

Hidden in the app, kept on the network

In every case I checked, farcaster.xyz didn’t show new casts from fids that haven’t signed, restricted or not. Public hubs still carry those casts.

Some restricted accounts still post through other clients. Of the 194 restricted on 18 Oct, 27 posted after the announcement, and at least 16 of them posted between two restricted reads. Of 255 restricted by 19 Oct ~22:00 UTC, 34 posted; 29 of those never minted VOTE later.

Of the same 194, 81% had cast in the 60 days before 15 Oct. After the announcement, 4 restricted fids made 52 casts with the farcaster.xyz key, all while the fid held VOTE (19 Oct).

These activity checks ran 18–19 Oct and were not rerun on the final 263.

What the data gives each side

For NS

  • These are app rules on an open protocol: public hubs keep the casts the app doesn’t show, and some restricted fids still post through other clients. ↑ Hidden in the app
  • Of the votes I could see, at most 98.3% are stay. ↑ Shares
  • Formal restrictions run in the hundreds: 263 at the last read, 5 of them carrying the operator’s test-account names. ↑ Hidden in the app
  • About half of all fids never had a farcaster.xyz key, and none of those signed. Among fids with 100+ app followers, 95% of sampled non-voters had been idle for 60+ days. ↑ Keys
  • Status can change: 10 fids once read restricted, and 57 of the 481 leavers, have since signed. ↑ Leave votes

For the Hypersnap fork

  • Signing puts your past posts under the rules (vote page) and is one-way for the signer. The vote page says you can change your vote later, but the app’s code shows no leave button after a stay vote, and revoking the token needs the operator’s signature. ↓ Method
  • Since 19 Oct, 09:36–09:44 UTC the API returns no leave votes (0 in 417,518 reads); the change was on the server and I found no announcement. ↑ Leave votes
  • In every case checked, the app shows no new casts from fids that haven’t signed, restricted or not. ↑ Hidden in the app
  • Where the key existed, few signed: ~1.7% of the ~1.1M fids above 300k with a farcaster.xyz key. Two thirds of fids with 10k+ hub followers show no vote. ↑ Shares
  • Restricted accounts include active ones: 81% of the 194 restricted on 18 Oct had cast in the 60 days before. ↑ Hidden in the app

Open343 signers come from one address’s batch of 758 fids. Who registered them, and why, can’t be told from public data.

How this was counted

Method and limits

Votes

Every VOTE Transfer event on Base, pulled twice from two providers. tokenId = fid. I found no public source for the VOTE contract. Its revoke reverts unless the operator signs, tested from the holder, the owner and a random address; 5 of its functions are unresolved.

Statuses

client.farcaster.xyz/v2/user?fid=N, logged out, latest read wins, read-only, one scanner at a time, ~7 requests/s in total. Plus 5 profile views in my own logged-in session, 20 Oct.

Coverage

Every fid that has cast, bar 1,125 failed reads and the 942 newest. 0 restricted among 42,849 never-cast fids read. 5,437 fids never returned a status, only 404s or failed reads, and are counted apart: the API gives no reason for a 404 or a restriction.

Hubs

haatz.quilibrium.com (Hypersnap’s public node) and snap.farcaster.xyz (a validator labelled Merkle).

App code

The farcaster.xyz web bundle and the vote page text, saved on 16, 19 and 20 Oct and compared. The opt-in endpoints are the same, and none resets or cancels a vote; after a stay vote the app offers no leave button.

How sure

Verified: two of my own methods or passes agree; no outside re-check yet. Estimate: a range or “~”. Status values in code type are the API’s own.
